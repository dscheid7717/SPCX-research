THE MODEL AGAINST THE MARKET
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
  At a 16.03% cost of equity (beta 2.216, my own estimate), this model says 58.23.
  At 14.02% (Blume-adjusted beta 1.815), it says 69.46.
  The market says 224.58 on September 24, 2026, on the same 24,514 million diluted shares.

  The range 58.23 to 69.46 comes from one input -- beta -- and
  nothing else. The question that leaves me is not whether NVIDIA is cheap.
  It is which of us is wrong about the discount rate: the market is pricing
  NVIDIA as though its cost of equity were far below what its own five-year
  return history implies. No recommendation is drawn here.
