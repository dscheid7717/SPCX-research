## Analyst-Generated Material

Statements labeled `UNSOURCED` in the report are the analyst's judgments, assumptions, interpretations, unknowns, research plans, or decision rules. They are intentionally not attributed to the company or treated as filing facts.

The approximate $(25.010) billion free-cash-flow figure is an analyst calculation using reported operating cash flow of $3.466 billion less reported purchases of property, plant, and equipment of $28.476 billion. Both inputs come from Source 5.

The approximately 82.7% AI share of consolidated capital expenditures is an analyst calculation using AI capital expenditures of $23.551 billion from Source 7 divided by consolidated capital expenditures of $28.476 billion from Source 5.
